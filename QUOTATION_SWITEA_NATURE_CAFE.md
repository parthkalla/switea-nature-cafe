## 1. Executive Summary

This proposal outlines the complete scope of engineering, design, and deployment delivered for **Switea Nature Cafe, Jaipur**. The solution replaces fragmented third-party digital menus with an end-to-end proprietary restaurant operating suite, encompassing a branded customer-facing web portal, contactless QR table ordering, real-time Kitchen Display System (KDS), administrative menu & inventory control, automated coupon/discount engine, direct media upload pipelines, and printable financial analytics.

---

## 2. Itemized Scope of Work & Deliverables

### Module 1: Brand Website & Public Web Portal
* **Custom UI/UX Design:** Bespoke nature/botanical aesthetic matching Switea Garden Cafe ambiance (lush greens, warm stone, elegant typography).
* **Core Pages:**
  * Dynamic Landing Page with Hero announcement, Story, and Features.
  * Interactive Ambiance Gallery with high-res photo masonry layout.
  * About & Cafe Philosophy section.
  * Contact & Location Page with integrated Google Maps, WhatsApp quick-connect, and business hours.
  * Customer Review submission & verified rating showcase.
* **SEO & Responsiveness:** 100% mobile, tablet, and desktop optimized with semantic schema markup.

### Module 2: Contactless Digital Menu & Table QR Ordering System
* **Table-Mapped QR Ordering:** Dynamic QR generation for individual tables with cryptographic table tokens (e.g., Table 01 - Table 10+).
* **Smart Digital Menu:**
  * Instant category navigation (Chai, Artisan Coffee, Maggi, Momos, Pizza, Coolers & Combos).
  * Veg/Non-Veg indicators, Bestseller & Chef’s Special tags, live search, and filter toggles.
  * Item modifiers & customisation (crust options, extra cheese, spice level, dip add-ons).
* **Interactive Cart & Smart Coupon Engine:**
  * Real-time bill calculation, taxes, and service charges.
  * Dynamic promo codes (e.g. `NATURE10`) with automated threshold validation and shortfall alerts.
* **Live Order Tracking:** Customer order status screen with dynamic progress (Received -> In Kitchen -> Ready -> Served) and post-service 5-star feedback form.

### Module 3: Kitchen Display System (KDS) & Order Management
* **Real-Time KDS Portal:** Dedicated kitchen screen with live ticket updates powered by WebSockets.
* **Order Lifecycle Workflow:** Real-time state transitions (Pending -> In Progress -> Ready -> Served -> Paid).
* **Visual & Audio Alerting:** Instant notifications upon new order arrival to ensure zero missed table orders.

### Module 4: Restaurant Administration & Inventory Management
* **Catalog Management:** Add, edit, reorder, and toggle availability of categories, items, and modifiers.
* **Direct Media & Image Upload Pipeline:** Integrated drag-and-drop file uploader with automated image optimization and live preview.
* **Table Tent QR Code Studio:** On-demand high-resolution printable table stands with embedded QR codes.
* **Staff & Role-Based Access Control:** Secure JWT authentication supporting Owner, Manager, Waiter, and Kitchen roles.
* **Customer Directory & Offer Builder:** Customer history logging and custom coupon creation.

### Module 5: Business Intelligence, Analytics & Reporting
* **Executive Dashboard:** Total revenue, daily order volumes, average ticket size, and active dining tables.
* **Visual Data Charts:** 7-Day Revenue Velocity bar graphs, channel breakdown (Dine-in vs Takeaway), and category share metrics.
* **Print-Optimized PDF Reports:** One-click financial statement generation with official Switea letterhead, formatted for A4 printing and accounting records.

### Module 6: SaaS Architecture, Super Admin & Cloud Infrastructure
* **Multi-Tenant Foundation:** Super Admin dashboard for multi-branch monitoring, subscription plans, and platform audit logs.
* **Deployment & Containerization:** Full Docker container configuration (`docker-compose.yml`), production-ready build bundles, and automated database migrations.
* **Source Code & Version Control:** Complete Git repository pushed to GitHub with production release archive.
